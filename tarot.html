A Biopunk Tarot Deck Generator

Seventy-eight cards of the old arcana, re-cultured in a wet lab. The Magician is a Splicer; Death is Apoptosis; the four suits are Plasmids, Cultures, Nucleases and Organs. Every seed grows a different strain of the deck — new palette, new procedurally drawn art, new mutations in the print run. Nothing here is loaded from a server; the whole deck is generated in your browser.

Pick a spread, then deal. Cards land face down — click one to flip it, or reveal the whole panel at once. Reversals are on by default; a reversed card is the same organism under different conditions.
