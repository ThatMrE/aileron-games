About Aileron

Do a
Barrel Roll

An aileron is the flap on an aircraft wing that makes it roll — the real aerodynamics behind “do a barrel roll.” We named the studio after it because that’s exactly what our games do: hide real, working science inside something you just want to play.

We’re a small, unapologetically nerdy studio making scientific games and novel interfaces. Every game turns a scientific concept into a mechanic — so you learn it by playing, not by studying. God Does Not Play Dice is genuine particle physics as a card duel. DNA Chess runs the Central Dogma of molecular biology. GeomeTree grows from a single line up to the Platonic solids. Wetware Arcana grows a whole 78-card tarot deck out of a single seed. Weji and Pun Bearable are playful experiments in what a game interface can even be.

Powered by curiosity. Every game is playable in your browser, right now, free.
