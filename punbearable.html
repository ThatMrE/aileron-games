How to Play

Ten Seconds. One Pun. No Mercy.

Players sit in a circle. A topic card is drawn. Going around, each player fires off a pun on that topic before the clock runs out — and the opponents are the ones counting you down.

01

Beat the Clock

You get 10 seconds to come up with a pun on the drawn topic. No pun, no dice.

02

They Count You Down

The opposing player (or players) counts down out loud. The pressure is the point.

03

Take Your Shame

Fail to land a pun or complete the mode’s objective, and you must accept the card as your shame.

“
What counts as a true pun?

A true pun is the misuse of a specific word in an improper context. Scoring is like golf — the more cards (points) you collect, the worse you’re doing. The player with the fewest cards wins; the one buried in shame loses.

Game Modes

Six Ways to Suffer

Same deck, wildly different games. Pick your poison and play until someone can’t bear it.

🎯

Free-for-All

The classic. Everyone for themselves, circle style. Fewest cards at the end wins.

💀

Elimination

Miss a pun and you’re out — if you’re vegetarian and make a missed-steak, you’re pout. Last punster standing wins.

⚔️

Head to Head

Two players, one topic, rapid-fire volleys back and forth until somebody blanks.

🍻

Drunk

Just add alcohol. The countdown feels shorter and the puns get… braver. Play responsibly.

👥

Teams

Squad up. Tag in your wordsmith when the topic favors them. Shame is shared.

⏱️

Time Attack

Two minutes to spit as many puns as you can on one topic — run dry and you take the card.
